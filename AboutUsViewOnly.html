Our Story

Our team of student developers created this system as part of our Capstone Project to address the common problem of difficulty in locating graves, especially in large cemetery areas.

By integrating a searchable database and an interactive map interface, we aim to provide a faster, more convenient, and stress-free experience for visitors.
